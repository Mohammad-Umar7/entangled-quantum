How it works

1 · Encode

Both photos are squared to 256 px and uploaded to Moth Atlas as assets.

2 · Teleport

Teleblur runs at 9 rotation strengths θ ∈ [0, 1], giving frames from A through scrambled to B.

3 · Decohere

Tessa sends each face's colours through a simulator with IBM Torino's noise.

4 · Echo

Your faces become a melody, which goes through a quantum-measured multi-tap delay.

5 · Measure

A single qubit is measured 100 times. Heads vote |A⟩, tails vote |B⟩; the majority is the collapsed state.

Honest note. Jobs run on Moth's simulators (Aer, plus a noise model calibrated to a real IBM chip), not on live QPU hardware, and nothing here needs a quantum computer to be fast. As Moth's CTO put it, the point is a new way of thinking about media: two images held in one quantum state, randomness with structure, echoes with negative amplitudes.
